✅ sprint4_valuation_module.py
   Location: /mnt/user-data/outputs/sprint4_valuation_module.py
   Size: ~8 KB
   Purpose: Valuation analysis engine
   Day: Day 26
   Features:
   - FCF Yield = (FCF / Market Cap) × 100
   - Sector Median P/E calculation
   - P/E vs Sector Median = ((P/E - Median) / Median) × 100
   - Valuation flags:
     * Caution: P/E > Sector Median × 1.5
     * Discount: P/E < Sector Median × 0.7
     * Fair: Otherwise
   - Output: valuation_summary.xlsx (92 rows, 10 columns)
   - Output: valuation_flags.csv (only Caution/Discount)
   - Full class: ValuationEngine
   - Logging and error handling
   - Standalone execution: python src/analytics/valuation.py
